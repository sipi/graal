# InteGraal

InteGraal is the successor of Graal by the same research group (BOREAL team), released under Apache-2.0, aimed at data integration with existential rules over heterogeneous sources. It reuses parts of Graal's design and adds data-source mappings, rule-base modules and benchmarking infrastructure.
