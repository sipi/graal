# Disputes log

The append-only log of challenged claims, notation proposals and their resolutions, for the whole domain reference. Each entry is also referenced from the *Disputes and open issues* section of the page it concerns, and the challenged claim carries the tag `[D]` until the entry is closed. Process: [conventions §8](conventions.md#8-contribution-and-challenge-process).

## Format

Add new entries at the end of the table. Never delete an entry; close it instead.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **#** | `DSP-<n>`, sequential. |
| **Date** | Date opened (ISO 8601). |
| **Page(s)** | Relative link(s) to the page(s) and section(s) concerned. |
| **Claim** | The exact claim challenged (quoted), or the notation proposal. |
| **Objection** | Why it is wrong, imprecise or contested. |
| **Evidence** | References, counter-examples, runs of systems (with versions). |
| **Status** | `open`, `resolved`, `rejected`, or `superseded by DSP-<n>`. |
| **Resolution** | For closed entries: what was changed, with date and evidence. |
